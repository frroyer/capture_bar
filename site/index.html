Why Takebox

Most capture tools stop you to ask where the file should go. Takebox never asks. It takes the shot, puts it on your clipboard, keeps it in the buffer, and is ready for the next one before you are.

Everything on one bar.

Takebox stays on top of every app, out of the way, and out of your captures. Every button is also a shortcut.

Questions

Is Takebox really free?

Yes, forever. No license, no account, no watermark and no time limit. Its updates are free too.

Is Takebox+ a subscription?

No. You pay €69 once and can use Takebox+ forever. The first year of updates is included. After that, another year of updates costs €17, whenever you want it.

What happens if I don't renew updates?

Nothing stops. The version you have keeps working forever. A newer release tells you it is outside your update year, and you keep using yours.

Do I pay before downloading?

No. Everyone downloads the same file. Start the 15-day Takebox+ trial from the app, with no card and no account. When it ends, the app goes back to free Takebox, and your demo packages stay readable.

Does the license key need internet?

No. Your key carries a signature that the app checks on your computer. Activating it sends nothing anywhere. Takebox only goes online if you turn on update checks.

Is there AI in it?

No generative AI. Nothing is written for you and nothing leaves your computer. Speech-to-text runs on your machine, only to time your words. If you want an assistant to help edit, you choose one and give it the package yourself.

How many computers can I use?

One license lets one person install 3 copies, for example at the office, on a laptop and at home. It is not 3 user licenses: each person who uses Takebox+ needs their own. Teams of five or more get a volume price.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 30 days of purchase.

When can I buy Takebox+?

Takebox+ is in development. Early access opens with the first build.
