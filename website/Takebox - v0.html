Why Takebox

Most capture tools stop you to ask where the file should go. Takebox never asks. It takes the shot, puts it on your clipboard, keeps it in the buffer, and is ready for the next one before you are.

Features

Everything on one bar.

Takebox stays on top of every app, out of the way, and out of your captures. Every button is also a shortcut.

123

Region, window or screen

Pick a mode once and it stays picked. Region reopens on the last selection, Window takes the one under your cursor, Screen takes the monitor you are on.

+3

A buffer, not a dialog

Captures stack up as thumbnails. It survives restarts and cleans itself after 7 days.

REC 00:42

Recording, in sync

Hardware H.264 to MP4, with your mic and system sound in one track. Pause skips cleanly.

You, in a bubble

A round webcam bubble you drag anywhere and resize. It is drawn into the video, never into your stills.

Mark it up

Arrows, boxes, pen, text, highlights, pixelate and crop. Layers stay editable with unlimited undo.

.GIF

GIF in one click

Turn any recording into a GIF from its card. Exports queue in the background with live progress.

Docks to any edge

Drop the bar near an edge and it snaps there, vertical on the sides. It fades to 40% when your cursor is elsewhere and remembers its spot per monitor layout.

Every monitor, every scale

Mixed DPI, screens plugged in mid-session, a picker when you have several. Captures stay at full native resolution.

Every control is reachable with Tab and named for Narrator, VoiceOver and Orca. English and French switch without a restart.

Private by design

Takebox works fully offline. Your captures stay on your disk, in a folder you choose, named the way you like.

Annotate

Point at it. Blur the rest.

Open any capture from its toast or its card. Shapes stay as layers until you save, and the original is kept, so you can always roll back.

Shortcuts

Your hands never leave the keys.

The same map on Windows, macOS and Linux. Every shortcut works from any app and can be remapped; clashes with other apps are flagged on the spot.

Press the letters on your keyboard. The rows light up.

  • Capture in the current modeCtrlShiftS
  • Start or stop recordingCtrlShiftR
  • Pause or resumeCtrlShiftP
  • Region, then captureCtrlShift1
  • Window, then captureCtrlShift2
  • Screen, then captureCtrlShift3
  • Open the bufferCtrlShiftB
  • Folder and file prefixCtrlShiftL
  • Show or hide the barCtrlShiftSpace

Under the hood

Rust, all the way down.

No WebView, no Electron, no garbage collector. Takebox talks straight to each system's own capture and encoder APIs, and never blocks the bar while it writes to disk.
